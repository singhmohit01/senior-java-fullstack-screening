# Intense Focus and Growth

One experience that stands out was when I took up the role of **SDET Lead for the Platform Solutions project for the first time**. I was responsible for building the overall test coverage, with a target of completing more than 200 test cases within approximately one month. It was a challenging timeline, so instead of immediately starting the work, I first spent time understanding the application, business processes, user roles, and different scenarios. I wanted to make sure that I was not simply completing a target, but producing work that would be useful and reliable for the team.

I stayed focused on this task throughout the month and continuously looked for ways to work more efficiently without compromising quality. I regularly reviewed my progress, identified areas that needed improvement, and adjusted my approach whenever required. There were several challenges along the way, but I remained committed to completing the task within the deadline. We eventually completed more than 200 test cases within the required timeframe. This experience helped me grow significantly, as it was my first opportunity to take ownership of work at this scale. More importantly, it taught me how to stay focused under pressure, manage my time, prioritize important work, and remain consistent until the goal was achieved.
